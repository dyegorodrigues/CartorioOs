## Rules
1. Alias does not automatically mean full synonymy.
2. Every alias should carry author/bank/context when meaning varies.
3. Homonyms become separate atoms with an explicit confusion edge.
4. A future study page should show aliases only where they prevent exam error; the full registry stays backstage.
